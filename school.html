Our story

A legacy of excellence on the Kenyan coast

Nestled in the vibrant heart of Mombasa, Badi Twalib Comprehensive School has been shaping exceptional young minds for nine years. Our name — meaning seekers of knowledge — defines our essence: rigorous academics, strong facilities and a nurturing community where every child's seed of greatness can blossom.

From the classrooms to the playing fields, from the science labs to the stage, our learners are taught not just to succeed, but to lead with purpose and serve with heart.

  • National Science Olympiad Champions 2025
  • Best Comprehensive School Award — Coast Region 2025
  • Regional Finalists, Under-16 Basketball
  • 1,000,000 mangroves planted through the 4K Club
